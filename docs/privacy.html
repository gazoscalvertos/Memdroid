Memdroid privacy policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

Memdroid is a free app, with no ads, for managing RAM, storage and battery on Android phones. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

The short version

Memdroid does not collect, store on a server, sell or share any personal data. Everything it reads stays on your phone. The app has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

What the app reads on your phone

  • Installed and running apps (Query all packages, Usage access), to show which apps use RAM, storage and battery, and when you last opened them.
  • Files and folders (All files access), to show what uses storage and to find large, old and duplicate files. Files are only deleted after you confirm, and they can be kept in an optional recycle bin on your phone for 30 days.
  • Battery statistics, through the optional Shizuku helper that you set up yourself.

This information is processed on your phone only. Usage history, settings and the recycle bin are stored in the app's private storage on your phone and are deleted when you uninstall Memdroid.

Things that leave your phone only when you choose

  • Bug reports, feature requests and crash reports open a pre-filled page on GitHub in your browser. They include the app version, phone model and Android version, plus anything you type. Nothing is sent unless you submit it on GitHub, where it is public.
  • Tips ("Buy me a beer") are handled by Google Play. Memdroid never sees your payment details. Google's privacy policy applies to the payment.
  • The heroes wall: after a tip you can choose to be listed by name, listed anonymously, or not listed. If you choose to be listed, a pre-filled public GitHub request is opened with the name you entered and the Play order ID, so the tip can be checked.

Children

Memdroid is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about privacy can be raised at github.com/gazoscalvertos/Memdroid/issues.
